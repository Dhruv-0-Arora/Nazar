# Ticket 2024-09-188: conference room monitor flickering

Opened: 2024-09-09 by office ops.
Status: resolved.

## Symptoms

The big monitor in the Jupiter conference room flickered during presentations.
The flicker only appeared when a laptop was connected over the long HDMI run.

## Investigation

The 10 m HDMI cable routed under the floor had a damaged shield.
Interference from the floor box power cabling caused intermittent signal loss.

## Resolution

Replaced the run with an active optical HDMI cable.
No flicker since.

## Lesson

For HDMI runs over 5 m, use active or optical cables.
