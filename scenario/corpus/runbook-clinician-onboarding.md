# Runbook: new clinician onboarding

Audience: practice managers and IT.
Last reviewed: 2026-03.

## Before the first day

- Practice manager files the access request with the clinician's role and site.
- IT creates the directory account and assigns the role group.
- Records access is granted by role, never per person.

## First day

Issue the badge and workstation, then walk through portal sign-in at the front desk.
Confirm the clinician appears in the provider dropdown before the first appointment block.

## Access reviews

Role membership is reviewed quarterly.
Any clinician who has not signed in for 90 days is suspended pending confirmation from their practice manager.
