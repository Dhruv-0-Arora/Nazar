# Ticket 2025-11-088: front desk printer jamming repeatedly

Opened: 2025-11-04 by reception.
Status: resolved.

## Symptoms

The front desk label printer jammed on roughly every third label.
Staff had cleared jams manually several times a day for a week.

## Investigation

The feed roller had worn and was no longer gripping the label stock consistently.
The print queue and drivers were fine; this was purely mechanical.

## Resolution

Replaced the feed roller under the service contract.
No further jams reported in the following month.
