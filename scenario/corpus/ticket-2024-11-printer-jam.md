# Ticket 2024-11-231: 2nd floor printer jams on duplex jobs

Opened: 2024-11-05 by office ops.
Status: resolved.

## Symptoms

The 2nd floor HP LaserJet jammed on every duplex print job.
Single-sided jobs printed fine, which confused everyone for a day.

## Investigation

The duplexer tray had a torn transport roller.
Paper weight above 90 gsm made the jam rate worse.

## Resolution

Facilities replaced the duplexer roller assembly under the service contract.
We also pinned a note to the printer asking people to use 80 gsm paper.

## Lesson

Check the duplexer first when only double-sided jobs fail.
