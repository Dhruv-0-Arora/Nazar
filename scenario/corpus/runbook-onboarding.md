# Runbook: new hire onboarding checklist

Audience: managers and IT.
Last reviewed: 2026-01.

## Before day one

- Create the corporate account and mailbox.
- Order a laptop and have IT image it with the standard build.
- Add the new hire to the team's chat channels and calendar.

## Day one

- Hand over the laptop and run through password setup and MFA enrollment.
- Walk through the acceptable use policy and have it signed.
- Introduce the buddy who answers questions in the first two weeks.

## First week

- Grant repository and VPN access after the security briefing.
- Schedule intro sessions with each neighbouring team.
- Confirm payroll and benefits enrollment is complete.
