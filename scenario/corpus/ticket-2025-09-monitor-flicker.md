# Ticket 2025-09-052: intermittent monitor flicker in triage

Opened: 2025-09-16 by triage nursing.
Status: resolved.

## Symptoms

Two monitors in triage flickered intermittently, more often in the afternoon.

## Investigation

Both monitors were on the same power strip as a portable heater.
The flicker correlated with the heater's duty cycle, not with anything on the network or the workstations.

## Resolution

Moved the heater to a separate circuit.
Flicker stopped and has not recurred.
